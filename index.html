    Questions

    Good to know

    Do I need to be a member to run?

    Yes. UNBND races are for club members. Register, pay the ₹999 membership fee and we'll activate your membership.

    How long does membership last?

    One year from when it's activated. Renew every year to keep running with the club.

    Are races free?

    Many races are free for active members, and some have an entry fee. Each race on the race schedule shows which.

    What is a race card?

    Your entry to one race. Generate it on the race schedule with your Club ID and registered mobile, save it to your phone, and show its QR code at the start line. Each race needs its own race card, and there is no entry without one, even for active members.

    Can I share my race card?

    No. Your race card's QR code is unique to you, so please don't post it on social media or send it to anyone. Organisers may ask for a government photo ID to confirm who you are.

    I've paid. When do I get my Club ID?

    Once we've checked your payment, we activate your membership and email your Club ID. Check your spam or promotions folder, or use Find my Club ID above.

    I'm a beginner. Can I join?

    Of course. Every pace is welcome, and nobody gets left behind.
